Landmark Cases

A constitution is what courts say it is. These twenty judgments built — and repeatedly rebuilt — the edifice of Indian constitutional law: the basic structure doctrine, due process under Article 21, federalism, affirmative action, privacy, gender justice and free speech.

Reading a case: Facts sets the dispute; Held states what the court decided; Significance explains why it matters for the Constitution. Citations follow the standard Indian reporter format (SCC = Supreme Court Cases; AIR = All India Reporter). Full judgments are available on the Supreme Court's website.
